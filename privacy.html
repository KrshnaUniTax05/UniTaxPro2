Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

1. Information We Collect

We collect account details (name, email), workspace configuration, and transaction data you enter into UniTax Pro.

Data is used to provide accounting, tax compliance, and approval workflow services. We do not sell personal data.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database with role-based access control. We apply encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Sharing

We share data only with authorized workspace members and as required by law or statutory compliance.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting your workspace administrator.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal local storage for session state. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
